Version 0.2.0 - July 14, 2026
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
- Simplified theme switching: Dark/Light/System theme are now single-click
  choices from the Settings menu and system tray, and the choice is saved
  across restarts. Replaces the old "Theme Settings" window.
- Added an in-app Help window (Help menu and system tray) covering opening
  files, keyword highlighting, sound alerts, theming, and updates.
- Update checker now shows a small hard-hat badge next to the app icon only
  when you're running a newer, unreleased build than the latest published
  release; a normal check or an available update just shows the usual icon.
  Fixes a bug where an available update could incorrectly show as an error.
- Reorganized bundled resources under assets/ (images, sounds) for
  consistency across Kranky Bear apps.
- Windows installer no longer bundles assets/images separately; those
  images are compiled directly into the executable.
